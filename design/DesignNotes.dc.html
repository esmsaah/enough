Design notes · for Claude Code

The audit reads like a receipt someone marked up for you.

01

Five screens are the reference

Landing, quick start, what we keep, usage card and result come from the first design pass. Every other screen copies their spacing, header and type. Screen padding 20, section gap 18, back arrow plus progress bar on top.

02

One set of example numbers

10 items, €1,488.04 a year, €124 a month. Five items flagged with one action each, €699.60 potential. Gym 3 visits and a €8 single entry gives the €192. Target €30 a month picks YouTube Premium, Disney+ and Netflix for €30.31.

03

Navy only for the big moments

Landing, the result header, the cut list header and the share card. Everything you work through sits on the light ground.

04

Lime means money you found

Used once per screen at most. The lime button on navy is the one exception the tokens allow.

05

Before paying

Total, monthly, item count, how many aren't earning their place, the saving as one number, and the first cut item in full. The rest are placeholder bars, not real rows hidden with blur. The withdrawal checkbox sits above the button.

06

Nothing found, nothing to pay

With no cut or look again item, or a saving under €10, the result turns into Good shape with a receipt that says TO PAY €0.00.

07

Verdicts carry a word and a shape

Cut is coral with a cross, Look again amber with a circle, Keep indigo with a tick. Group totals are savings for Cut and Look again, cost for Keep.

08

Word "free" is gone

Titles, links and states were checked. "Not now" and "Delete everything" replace the old links.

Open for the founder Section 1 of the brief says the person never sees which items before paying, section 9 shows the first cut item. The screens follow section 9. The withdrawal wording, founder name and domain are still placeholders.
