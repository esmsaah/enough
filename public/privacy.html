Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version. Your bank statement is read on your own device and never uploaded. We don't have accounts, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything about you.

Your statements

When you add a statement, your browser reads it on your device. Names, account numbers, IBANs, addresses and balances are removed before anything is saved. The statement file and its amounts, dates and personal details never leave your device.

What stays on your device

Your answers, the services you picked and your result are saved in your browser's own storage, so you can come back where you left off. "Delete everything" removes all of it at once.

What can reach our server

  • Shop names, if you allow it. With "Use AI to recognise shop names" on, we send only shop names (for example "SPOTIFY P2F8") to recognise what they are. Never amounts, dates or your details. You can turn this off before adding a statement.
  • An anonymous list of services (names, categories and your usage answers) to improve the advice. It carries no name, email or bank data.
  • Your email, only if you ask for reminders or a copy of your list. We use it only to send what you asked for, and you can unsubscribe from any email.

Shop names may be processed by Anthropic (the AI provider) on our behalf. Our site runs on Cloudflare.

Payment

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our reseller and merchant of record. They collect your payment details and email for the purchase under their own privacy policy. We only receive confirmation that the payment went through.

No tracking

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no cookies for tracking.

Your rights

You can delete everything on your device yourself. For anything else, including your email or a question, write to esma.hodz@gmail.com.
